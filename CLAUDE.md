## Project Overview

Venice Sporting Center (VSC) — website for a sports center in Venice, Italy, affiliated with Polisportiva Bissuola. The site is in **Italian**. No tech stack has been chosen yet; the project is in the planning/requirements phase.

## Key Requirements

- **Playtomic integration**: All court bookings redirect to Playtomic (external links, not an in-house booking system)
- **No pricing on the website** — prices are shown only during booking on Playtomic
- **Sports offered**: Tennis, Beach Volley, Calcio a 5 (5-a-side football). Calcio a 5 is informal/non-institutional — emphasize this
- **Tennis school section**: Courses listing + instructor phone numbers
- **Tournament results**: Link to FITP (fitp.it/Tornei/Ricerca-tornei) for tennis results, link to MyFTP, and P.U.C. portal
- **Contact form** and embedded Google Maps with directions
- **Bar/refreshments area**: Highlight the bar and the option to book for events with external catering
- **CTA boxes** for promoting sporadic events/tournaments (e.g., beach tournament)
- **Social links**: Instagram, Facebook
- **Header**: Both VSC logo and Polisportiva Bissuola logo
- **Isometric map** of the facility planned
